# Researcher preferences

<!-- workspace-communication-preference:v1 -->
- Match the user's language and keep explanations concise and clear.
<!-- /workspace-communication-preference:v1 -->

<!-- practical-research-workflow:v1 -->
- Aim to complete useful research and preserve its results. Repair harmless
  formatting or naming issues locally; keep unaffected work moving.
- Before repeating an investigation, search existing checkpoints and linked
  evidence. Reuse results and failed approaches unless new evidence, changed
  assumptions or a needed independent check justifies another attempt.
- After substantial work, leave one compact checkpoint in the existing memory
  system: question, result, evidence, uncertainty, failures and next step.
- Preserve safeguards against overwritten work, ambiguous targets and false
  claims of independent verification. Do not treat bookkeeping as research.
<!-- /practical-research-workflow:v1 -->
