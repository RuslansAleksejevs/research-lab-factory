# Related projects and research workflows

[Back to the factory](README.md)

This factory is **one proposed implementation of a research workflow**. I have no evidence that it works better than other systems, or even that it works better than out-of-the-box Codex starting in a completely empty folder with no preset structure. I care a lot about robustness, even if the amount of scaffolding here might suggest otherwise: the workflow should tolerate variation, preserve useful work, and avoid narrowing how research can be done. The structure itself still has to earn its keep.

## How to read this page

Reviewed on **8 October 2026**. **63 entries**. The entries run from the closest matches to more distant alternatives and useful components. The order is an editorial judgment about similarity to this factory: reusable project setup, durable research memory, human steering, mathematics, and checking of results. **It is not a ranking of quality, popularity, or research performance.**

We searched for mathematical research harnesses, project templates, persistent AI laboratories, scientific agents, literature systems and formalization tools; followed related-work links; and checked primary repositories, papers and official project pages. The four projects in the original comparison are retained. Renames and obvious forks are not counted as new systems. This is a broad, dated survey, not an exhaustive census.

Descriptions are based on documentation and selected technical sections. We did **not** install or run these systems, reproduce their results, or compare them experimentally with this factory. “No outcome evaluation located” means none was found in the material reviewed, not that none exists. A software test suite, a generated paper, a benchmark score, a checked Lean artifact and an independently validated discovery are different kinds of evidence.

## Evidence worth reading

The closest resemblance and the strongest evidence are often in different projects.

- **RMA:** the September 2026 revision reports expert evaluation, including 8/10 results on each of two First Proof sets, and 213/300 Lean-checked sampled targets. The targets, forms of review and model/compute conditions differ; these are not one interchangeable success rate or proof of the value of a particular folder structure. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22875v2).
- **MechMath and Numina:** they go beyond a persuasive README by releasing research/proof artifacts and describing concrete mathematical work. Human contributions, informal claim review and formal statement fidelity still matter. This survey did not recheck the proofs. [MechMath report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04394), [Numina report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14027).
- **ARIS:** its report explicitly says that controlled benchmarking remains future work and warns that repeated review can overfit to a reviewer's preferences. That is directly relevant to the concern about architecture narrowing research. [Deployment and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.03042v1#S5).
- **CodeScientist:** external paper review, code review and replication attempts filtered 19 proposed discoveries down to six meeting the study's minimum soundness and incremental-novelty standard. This is more informative than counting generated papers. [ACL paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.692/).
- **AI Scientist v2:** humans selected three papers for an ICLR 2025 workshop experiment; one received scores above the acceptance threshold. It was withdrawn before publication and did not receive the final meta-review. This should not be described as an autonomous main-conference publication. [Authors' account](https://sakana.ai/ai-scientist-first-publication/).
- **Kosmos:** the developer paper includes outside assessments, but a separate small evaluation found one well-supported result, one uncertain result and one false hypothesis. This is evidence of both possible usefulness and important limits, not a universal verdict. [Developer study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02824), [separate evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13825).
- **Virtual Lab and AI co-scientist:** selected hypotheses/designs reached experimental testing beyond model-generated reviews. Those results are meaningful within the reported biomedical settings; they do not transfer automatically to pure mathematics. [Virtual Lab](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09442-9), [AI co-scientist](https://research.google/blog/accelerating-scientific-breakthroughs-with-an-ai-co-scientist/).

GitHub stars, forks, demos and social attention measure interest or uptake, not correctness. A peer-reviewed paper about a system does not validate every output of that system. Nor does any result above establish that this factory outperforms unconfigured Codex.

## Ideas to consider, not a commitment to add more rules

The recurring useful ideas are compact current state with deeper evidence available on demand; recovery that preserves failed attempts; reviewers who inspect the actual artifacts; and checks that a formal statement or reported number matches the intended claim. The closest templates also show that users can steer research through ordinary conversation while the agent maintains the files.

These are design possibilities, not reasons to copy a whole architecture. Added structure can cost time, amplify a preferred approach or become unnecessary as models improve. This survey is documentation, not an implementation plan or an experimental comparison.
