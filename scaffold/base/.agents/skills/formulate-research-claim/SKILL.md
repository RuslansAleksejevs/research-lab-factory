# Formulate Research Claim

Write one exact typed statement. List equivalent-looking variants separately,
identify missing definitions and boundary cases, apply `LAB_PROFILE.json`
checks, and assign `UNKNOWN` or `PLAUSIBLE_UNVERIFIED`. Do not silently choose a
stronger, weaker, or more convenient theorem.
