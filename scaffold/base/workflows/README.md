# Workflows

- `hard_math_search_orchestration.md`: `LIGHT`/`FULL` proof and refutation
  search, mechanism families, gap strength, attacks, lineage, and closure.
- `proof_protocol.md`: exact-target proof lifecycle.
- `literature_protocol.md`: primary-source and terminology audit.
- `research_directions_protocol.md`: small ranked direction portfolio.
- `self_improvement_protocol.md`: human-gated, eval-backed architecture changes.

<!-- laboratory-survey-protocol:v1 -->
- `laboratory_survey_protocol.md`: whole-laboratory wake-up survey, evidence
  reconciliation, freshness control, generalization frontier, optional explicit
  literature delta, and ranked next-round portfolio.
<!-- /laboratory-survey-protocol:v1 -->

<!-- fleet-research-round-protocol:v1 -->
- `research_round_protocol.md`: read-only fleet proposals and collection,
  immutable user-approved run plans, lab-local research execution, mandatory
  checkpoints, bounded projections, retries, and architecture drift guards.
<!-- /fleet-research-round-protocol:v1 -->

<!-- laboratory-stewardship-protocol:v1 -->
- `laboratory_stewardship_protocol.md`: deep whole-laboratory audit, finite
  staged repair transaction, evidence-gated reconciliation, validation, and a
  stable bound final survey.
<!-- /laboratory-stewardship-protocol:v1 -->

- `claude_terminal_protocol.md`: optional bounded Claude sessions operated by
  Codex, same-session resume, checked collection, preservation of partial
  results, and intake into the existing research memory.


Optional modules add paper, talk, computation/formalization, and external-model
workflows. The formalization module offers a pilot-first Lean Room default with separate
build, trust-boundary, semantic-correspondence, and refactor gates. Workflows
route activity; their planning defaults do not constrain mathematical method,
and they do not establish truth by themselves.
