# Literature Protocol

Prefer primary sources. Apply `sources/TRUST_TIERS.md` to provenance and
authority, independently of relevance and claim status. Record date, query,
source, theorem or proposition number, edition or version, printed/PDF page
convention, and whether the exact statement was inspected. Separate exact
matches, related results, methodology, terminology variants, and unverified
leads. A high source tier never substitutes for checking the theorem's exact
hypotheses and use in the project.
