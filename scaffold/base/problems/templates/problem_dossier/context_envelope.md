A complete solution must prove or refute exactly the frozen target, close every
load-bearing obligation, survive `attack_surface.md`, and pass independent
verification. Decisive proof certification requires an `I3 BLIND` verifier, a
stable target ID/artifact/SHA-256, canonical candidate ID/SHA-256, structured
originating/material-repair run IDs, and typed hash-bound exposure, report, and
closure manifests. Verified closure also requires pairwise-distinct
proof-obligation, source-audit, and attack-exit evidence with role-specific
manifests, a complete dependency snapshot, and one audit packet bound unchanged
through exposure, the authoritative structured verifier-report manifest, and
closure. The prose report must repeat and agree on the canonical verdict fields.

current snapshot. It does not prove the mathematics, authenticate runs or
authorship, or prove that the stated information boundary was respected.
