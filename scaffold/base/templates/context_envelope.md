## Completion Contract

A complete solution must:

- prove or refute exactly the frozen target;
- close every load-bearing obligation by proof, exact primary-source import,
  formal verification, or a clearly identified computational certificate;
- survive the problem-derived attack surface and, for a proof candidate, a
  decisive `I3 BLIND` verification;
- bind the stable target ID/artifact/SHA-256 and one canonical candidate ID and
  SHA-256 across route, lineage, typed exposure/report/closure manifests, and
  verifier records, with a verifier run ID disjoint from all originating,
  material-repair, and historical nondecisive audit run IDs;
- use pairwise-distinct proof-obligation, source-audit, and attack-surface-exit
  evidence artifacts with target/candidate-bound typed manifests;
- freeze the complete candidate ancestry in a dependency snapshot, bind it and
  all role evidence into one audit packet, and bind that same packet through
  exposure, structured verifier report, and closure;
- treat the verifier-report manifest as the authoritative structured workflow
  record while requiring its prose report to repeat and agree on verifier
  identity/independence, verdict, highest gap, objections, and repairs;
- preserve all hypotheses and typing data.

These bindings establish current-record consistency and content identity, not
mathematical truth or provenance authenticity. They do not by themselves prove
the theorem, authenticate run IDs, or prove that an exposure boundary held.

## Non-Solutions and Anti-Goals

Record outputs that may be useful but do not solve the frozen target:

- special cases that do not imply the full statement;
- reformulations or reductions with an equivalent-strength missing lemma;
- fixed-range computations without a general certificate;
- analogies, heuristic evidence, or repeated LLM agreement;
- reductions to an unproved theorem of comparable strength;
- a proof of a silently weakened or differently typed statement.

## Hypothesis and Statement Change Control

No agent may silently alter this envelope. A useful theorem under changed
hypotheses is recorded as a separate candidate. Update the envelope version and
changelog before treating a changed statement as the main target.

## Final Status Rule

The allowed terminal outcomes are:

1. a complete proof with decisive `I3 BLIND` audit, or an independently checked
   refutation; or
2. the strongest rigorously established residual result, its exact unresolved
   gap, and the preserved registry of blocked/refuted routes.

Outcome 2 is not a solution of the frozen target.
