## Conventions

- Priorities: `P0` urgent, `P1` important, `P2` useful, `P3` someday.
- When asked, show `NOW`, then `NEXT`, then `LATER`; mention relevant `BLOCKED` items separately.
- Give new entries the next available `B-NNN` ID.
- Update an entry's wording and next action when its scope changes. Move completed entries to `Completed`, preserving their history.
