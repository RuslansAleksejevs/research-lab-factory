# Research Lab Practices

Status: living laboratory note.

- Separate raw sources, digests, proof attempts, verifier reports, and polished
  exposition.
- Convert reusable constructions and recurring failures into auditable
  artifacts and evals.
- Maintain enough maps and summaries to resume after a long gap.
- Promote generic architecture improvements back to the template only through
  an explicit, eval-backed proposal; never copy live research state upstream.
