# Research contract

The active contract is `AGENTS.md`; the method is an independently versioned
research choice. Source/capture completeness, AI origin, interpretation,
originality and soundness are separate assessments. Neither a vote nor a hash
promotes a claim to truth. No mathematical proof/Lean gate is installed.

Read-only AI critics receive an explicit question and exact source/contribution
versions. Record their actual session/role and observations, including unknowns.
They do not approve publication, alter provenance, or erase disagreement.
